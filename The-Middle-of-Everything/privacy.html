Privacy

This website is made by followorbounce. It has no accounts, no comments, no forms and no shop, and it never asks visitors for personal information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read, so we can make the site better. It stores cookies and sends Google technical details such as the page address, browser type, screen size, approximate region and a random visitor ID. On this site, Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off. We don't use any advertising or remarketing tags, and we don't sell or share data. Google's own policy explains how it handles this data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can block analytics with a browser's tracking protection or with Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

Fonts and videos

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Episode videos, once they are published, play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which doesn't store YouTube cookies until a video plays.
