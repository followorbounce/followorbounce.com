Cathedral

Chris goes into a scraped knee to help. The knee has it under control.

Pim has a fever, a scraped knee and a theory: there's a city in it. With bells. A neighbour is on duty for the night, and Chris goes in to help. The knee has it under control.

A burst water main calls Rosa out for the night, so Delphine is on duty (“Rule one: I do not sleep on duty”) while Pim runs a fever. Chris reads one fact online, Ori corrects it by phone, and Chris falls into Pim's healing knee to help. Inside is a cathedral that files everything, including Chris, under “unclear,” and someone has been there before.

What's real: a body has roughly as many bacteria as human cells (about 38 trillion to 30 trillion), not ten times more, and a fever is the body raising its set point on purpose. The cathedral, its bells and doors are a picture, not anatomy.

Did you spot Ninety-One? It's hiding somewhere in this episode.
