Crossroads

A secret, a turning planet, and a game called Where Am I.

Hollis tells Chris a secret, and Ori and June's stargazing plans depend on it. Chris swore to keep it. Then Chris falls onto a turning planet and loses track of where they are.

Chris swears on the middle of everything to keep Hollis's secret, and then has to watch their friends plan a night that won't happen. In the library map room Legend keeps the world's crossroads traditions on separate panels. Then Chris falls up onto a turning planet, where knowing where you are means knowing when you are, and finds a trail of footprints that never stop.

What's real: Earth turns once in 23 h 56 min against the stars, 15 degrees an hour; the height of Polaris gives your latitude; and finding longitude at sea needed an accurate clock (a sea watch was tested in 1761–62). The crossroads panels show separate traditions, each attributed to its own culture.

Did you spot Ninety-One? It's hiding somewhere in this episode.

For grown-ups

About keeping a promise when it hurts other people, with real navigation history (Polaris, longitude and time) and crossroads traditions from Greek and Roman, Yoruba and Japanese culture, each kept separate and named for its own culture.
