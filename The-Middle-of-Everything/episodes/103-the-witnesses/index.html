The Witnesses

Chris is sure they saw it happen. Hollis has a card for that.

A birdbath is broken. Three witnesses. Three different stories. Chris is certain they saw it happen. Hollis has a card for that.

Delphine's thirty-one-year-old birdbath is in pieces and Wendell's weather balloon is the obvious suspect. Chris says they saw it happen. At the Residents' Hearing, Hollis hands out cards: green for what you saw, yellow for what you think, and a separate card for what you think of a person. Then Chris falls into the crack in the bowl, and the crack tells its own story.

What's real: water expands as it freezes, which can widen a crack from the inside, and fracture surfaces carry ridges that fan out from where the crack began.

Did you spot Ninety-One? It's hiding somewhere in this episode.

For grown-ups

About the difference between what you saw, what you think and what you feel about someone, told through a neighbours' dispute over a broken birdbath; it touches gently on an older neighbour's grief, and Chris is grounded for being on a roof.
