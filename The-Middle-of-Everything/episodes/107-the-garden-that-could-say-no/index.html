The Garden That Could Say No

A garden that has to love you, and the day it gets to choose.

Chris draws a garden where every flower has to love Wendell. Wendell, recovering in hospital, asks a weatherman's question: do they have a choice?

Wendell is in hospital after heart surgery, so Chris makes him a map-garden where every flower-being loves him, exactly as written. It's a bit much. Wendell asks a weatherman's question (do they have a choice?) and Chris rewrites the key. Some flowers stay, some squabble, and one walks off the map. Then Chris asks the grown-ups the big question, and gets three honest, different answers.

What's real: a coronary bypass gives blood a new route around a narrowed artery. Wendell's, Rosa's and Ori's grandad's views are their own beliefs, shown as beliefs.

Did you spot Ninety-One? It's hiding somewhere in this episode.

For grown-ups

About why love has to be free to mean anything, around a family friend's recovery from heart bypass surgery: a churchgoer, a non-believer and a friend whose grandad reads the Book of Job each give their own answer as belief, not fact, and nobody's illness is anyone's fault.

A yes you can't refuse isn't a yes.
