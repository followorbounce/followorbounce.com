Pim's Wall

A red tie, a rainstorm and a hidden mountain nobody drew.

Pim's wall says cereal makes it Tuesday and a red tie makes it rain. Chris and Ori test the wall for a school project and find the hidden cause behind a coincidence.

Pim's bedroom wall is a crayon map of everything that's connected. For a school project, Chris and Ori test it: five heads in a row, Wendell's red tie and the rain, the dog and the post. Inside a graph, Legend shows them the hidden mountain that makes two things look linked. Meanwhile Hollis gets a hat, and a survey van keeps driving the same route.

What's real: five heads in a row on a fair coin is about 1 in 32, and a hidden third cause (a confounder) can make two unrelated things rise together, like ice-cream sales and pool rescues in hot weather.

Did you spot Ninety-One? It's hiding somewhere in this episode.
